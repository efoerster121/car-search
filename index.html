About RS Spyder wheels

RS Spyder Design comes in 20" or 21" sizes. Stock wheels are usually 19" or 20" cast. If a car has them it's usually called out in the spec line. If not, retrofit OEM sets run ~$5,800 (20") to ~$7,950 (21") at Porsche Pittsburgh — budget for a swap if you find an otherwise-perfect car.
